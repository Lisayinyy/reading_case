# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

People studying open research on large language models, especially readers who need to move from a large paper collection into sustained, focused reading.

## Product Purpose

Lisa's World is a browser-based reading room for open LLM papers. The first surface makes one paper feel present and approachable, while keeping a small, useful reading queue within reach.

## Positioning

An academic reading interface that uses light to direct attention instead of asking the reader to fight a noisy research feed.

## Brand Personality

Quietly cinematic, precise, and curious. The experience should feel like opening a research desk late at night: intimate without becoming ornamental.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic academic dashboards, dense card walls, productivity-app gradients, and visual effects that obscure the paper itself.

## Design Principles

- Let a single reading task dominate the viewport.
- Make the light effect explain focus, rather than serve as decoration.
- Keep scholarly metadata compact, clear, and useful.
- Preserve legibility when motion is reduced or the lamp is off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The reading surface remains readable without pointer interaction, supports keyboard focus, exposes controls with labels, and reduces non-essential animation for people who request reduced motion.
